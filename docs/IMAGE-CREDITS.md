## Tips for a more premium look

1. Prefer soft neutrals, natural light, and shallow depth of field — matches the cream/blush brand.
2. Avoid busy salon clutter and heavy neon for this aesthetic.
3. For launch, replace stock with the technician’s real work (phone photos are fine if well-lit and cropped consistently).
4. Keep aspect ratios: hero ~16:10, portfolio ~4:5, Instagram 1:1, reviews 1:1.
